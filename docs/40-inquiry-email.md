**Subject:** Upcoming programs — open-source spending controls for agentic payments on XRPL

Hello RippleX Ecosystem team,

I am an independent developer based in Japan, currently building an open-source
project on the XRP Ledger. I noticed that there are presently no open calls for
applications, and that additional programming is due to be announced in October
2026, so I wanted to introduce the work early rather than wait.

**What I am building.** Most of the tooling released so far — including the XRPL AI
Starter Kit and the x402 / MPP integrations — solves how an agent *makes* a payment.
I am working on the opposite side: how a human *constrains* what an agent is allowed
to pay for. Specifically, using Payment Channels so that the spending ceiling is
enforced by the ledger rather than by application code, and Permissioned Domains
(XLS-80) with Credentials (XLS-70) so that an agent can only pay counterparties that
a human has pre-approved.

I would appreciate your guidance on three points:

1. **Upcoming programs** — Is there a mailing list or other channel where the October
   announcement will be posted? I would like to make sure I do not miss the window.

2. **Fit** — Would this direction fit the grant program's current priorities, or is it
   better suited to a different part of the ecosystem? I would rather hear that early
   than submit something misaligned.

3. **Growth milestones** — The FAQ notes that roughly 70% of an award is tied to
   growth-based milestones using on-chain metrics. For developer-facing infrastructure,
   where success is measured in integrations rather than end users, how are those
   milestones typically defined?

I am happy to share the repository and a short demo once the core is working. Thank
you for your time.

Best regards,
